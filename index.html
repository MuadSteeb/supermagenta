Your browser needs canvas support to play the marble maze.

Move your mouse toward an edge or corner to lift that part of the maze. Gravity rolls the marble downhill, away from the raised side. Move toward the center to level the maze, or lift the opposite side to brake. Reach the glowing ring. On a touchscreen, drag to tilt. You can also focus the maze and use the arrow keys to lift a side. Press R or Escape to start again. The maze resets automatically after you reach the ring.
